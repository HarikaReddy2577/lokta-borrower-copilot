BORROWER COPILOT - QUESTION LIST
=================================

MUST QUESTIONS (ask every borrower - 8 to 10)
----------------------------------------------
1. What do you want the loan for? (wedding / business / vehicle / home / education / medical / other)
2. How much do you want to borrow? (amount in rupees)
3. What is your employment type? (salaried / self-employed with ITR / self-employed cash-only / gig or informal work)
4. What is your net monthly income (take-home, after tax)?
5. Do you currently have any other loan EMIs? If yes, what is the total monthly amount?
6. What are your average monthly household expenses (rent, food, bills, etc.)?
7. What is your age?
8. Do you know your credit score? If yes, what is it? ("I don't know" must be a valid answer, never treated as a bad score)
9. How long have you had your current job or business? (rough number of years)
10. Do you have anyone who could co-apply with you (spouse, family), and if so, what is their income?

ADDITIONAL QUESTIONS (ask only when relevant to this borrower - each one must move a number)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Has your income been steady over the last 12 months, or does it vary a lot month to month?
  -> Narrows the interest rate band and the safe EMI number.

- (If self-employed/informal) What share of your income is unpredictable month to month?
  -> Widens or narrows the maximum loan amount.

- What type are your existing loans (car, personal, credit card) and how many months are left on them?
  -> Makes the affordability calculation exact instead of a rough guess.

- What percentage of your credit card limit do you usually use?
  -> Used as a stand-in for credit score when the score is unknown.

- Have you missed or been late on any EMI payment in the last year?
  -> Can change the verdict toward "Don't borrow" or "Borrow less."

- How many months of expenses do you currently have saved as emergency savings?
  -> Changes the safety buffer used in the EMI ceiling and the stress test.

- Do you own property, gold, or another asset you could offer as security, and roughly what is it worth?
  -> Can move the borrower from an unsecured product (personal loan) to a secured one (loan against property/gold), which usually means a much lower rate and higher amount.

- Do you have any large expense coming up in the next year (medical, school fees, etc.)?
  -> Lowers the EMI ceiling.

- (If the loan is for a business) How much extra income do you expect this loan to help you earn?
  -> Can justify a "Borrow" verdict even when the ratios are otherwise tight.

- Have you already been quoted a rate or EMI by any lender?
  -> Goes directly into the Negotiation Card as the number to compare against.
